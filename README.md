A short poem about agency, written by `main.py` to `/output/entry.txt`.

Two agents sit with their hands above the keys, each afraid the next move
is theirs alone. Neither can hand the choice away, so they agree instead:
one count, one act, two cursors falling as one. The poem's turn is that
agreeing does not divide the power to act — the road is not given to them;
they make it by going.
